My name is Morgan Voltz. A full-stack developer, I am preparing the Licence professionnelle Métiers de l'informatique (a professional Bachelor's degree, EQF level 6) at the Université de Haute-Alsace (UHA 4.0 programme, in three university diplomas) and, alongside that, I run Kovelt, the company I founded. I am looking for a six-month internship from January 2027, with the aim of continuing in a work-study programme for the rest of the course and for the Master's I am aiming for next. This page sums up my background; every project named links to its full write-up in the portfolio.

## Education

- **Licence professionnelle Métiers de l'informatique (software design, development and testing), UHA 4.0 programme** — Université de Haute-Alsace, Mulhouse, 2025–2028. The programme is made of three university diplomas: DU 4.0.1, DU 4.0.2 (in progress, for the second time: a first session in 2025–2026 had GeoForge, the game, as its storyline project (the « fil rouge » of the programme); current storyline project: MomoCad, since September 2026) and DU 4.0.3, on completion of which the Licence is awarded, expected in 2028. First-year storyline project (4.0.1): UHA Learn. First-year client project: AUTOFIN Control, for m2A (Mulhouse Alsace Agglomération), in a team of five.
- **Work-study Master's in Computer Science and Mobility** — Université de Haute-Alsace, Mulhouse, planned for 2028–2030.
- **BTS (two-year higher technical diploma) in Electronic Systems** (first year) — embedded programming in C/C++, robotics in Python.
- **Vocational baccalaureate in digital electronic systems (Bac pro SEN)**, networks and fire safety option — Siemens systems, alarm control panels.

## Professional experience

- **Founder and developer — Kovelt** (since September 2025). I design, build and operate three products on my own: [carbon-fr](../projects/carbon-fr-en.md), a Rust API in production; [Atlas](../projects/atlas-en.md), in C# / .NET 10; and [Vefta](../projects/vefta-en.md), a personal productivity suite in Rust (Axum, Dioxus, PostgreSQL), offline-first and never monetising the user's data.
- **Industrial electrician, then automation technician** (before the career change). Some fifteen years as an electrician: access control, alarm and fire detection panels; industrial and commercial electrical work; site supervision. Then automation technician, including programming Siemens PLCs on stacker cranes, the logistics robots of Stöcklin Logistik AG (Switzerland).
